The 2017 edition of the _Vampire: The Eternal Struggle_ **European Championship** was played in **Berlin** (Germany), within World of Darkness Berlin: Day 1 on Saturday 13 May (**155 players**), Day 2 on Sunday 14 (**40**).

European Champion: **Danilo T.** (Italy). Day 1 was won by **François G.** (France).
