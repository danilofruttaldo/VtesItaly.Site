The 2011 edition of the _Vampire: The Eternal Struggle_ **European Championship** was played in **Warsaw** (Poland) from 11 to 13 November 2011. European Champion: **Otso S.** (Finland).

Side events: the Last Chance Qualifier was won by **Gyula E.** (Hungary), the Legendary Vampire by **Tomasz I.** (Poland), the First Chance Qualifier by **Djordje B.** (Serbia). Archon records only the winner of these tournaments, as of Day 2.
