The 2013 edition of the _Vampire: The Eternal Struggle_ **European Championship** was played in **Stockholm** (Sweden): Day 1 on Saturday 5 October (**99 players**), Day 2 on Sunday 6 (**40**).

European Champion: **Hugh A.** (United Kingdom), with a Ventrue Stickman deck. Day 1 was won by **Martin W.** (Austria).
