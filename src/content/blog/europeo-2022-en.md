The 2022 edition of the _Vampire: The Eternal Struggle_ **European Championship** was played in **Helsinki** (Finland), the first after two years stopped by COVID: the Last Chance Qualifier on Friday 29 July, Day 1 on Saturday 30 (**99 players**), Day 2 (**50**) and the Best of the Rest on Sunday 31.

European Champion: **Otso S.** (Finland). Day 1 was won by **Antti P.** (Finland).
