In November 2005 a group of Italian players set off for **Budapest**, host of the _Vampire: The Eternal Struggle_ **European Championship**.

The tournament was played on **Sunday, November 6**, in a hall full of tables and of Methuselahs from all over Europe. Around it, a week of travel from November 2 to 7: the **Parliament** on the Danube, the **Chain Bridge**, the **Fisherman's Bastion**, the **Central Market Hall**, **Heroes' Square**, the labyrinth beneath Buda Castle and, in the evenings, dinners of goulash and beer halls.

Twenty years later we found the photos of those days: you can browse them below and in the community photo archive.
