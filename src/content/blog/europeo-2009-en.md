The 2009 edition of the _Vampire: The Eternal Struggle_ **European Championship** was played in **Palma de Mallorca** (Spain): the Last Chance Qualifier on Friday 13 November, Day 1 on Saturday 14 (**50 players**), Day 2 on Sunday 15.

European Champion: **Martin W.** (Austria), with a Toreador antitribu bleed & bounce deck. Day 1 was won by **Vincent R.** (France).
