The 2015 edition of the _Vampire: The Eternal Struggle_ **European Championship** was played in **Warsaw** (Poland): Day 1 on Saturday 29 August (**95 players**), Day 2 on Sunday 30 (**40**).

European Champion: **Marcin W.** (Poland), with a Goratrix wall. Day 1 was won by **Selim A.** (France).
