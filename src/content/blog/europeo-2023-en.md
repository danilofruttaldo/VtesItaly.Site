The 2023 edition of the _Vampire: The Eternal Struggle_ **European Championship** was played in **Barcelona** (Spain) from 13 to 15 October 2023: the Last Chance Qualifier on Friday (**221 players**), Day 1 on Saturday (**269**), Day 2 on Sunday (**85**).

European Champion: **Jonas S.** (Sweden), with a Ministry Anarch stealth/bleed deck. Day 1 was won by **Otso S.** (Finland).
