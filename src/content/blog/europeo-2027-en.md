The **2027 European Championship** of _Vampire: The Eternal Struggle_ is played in **Gelsenkirchen**, in the Ruhr (Germany), **from 29 to 31 October 2027**. The venue was announced in Vilnius, during EC 2026.

- **Week of Nightmares**, Monday 25 to Thursday 28: tournaments and casual play.
- **Friday 29**: side event, included in the EC ticket.
- **Saturday 30 and Sunday 31**: Day 1 and Day 2, 3 rounds + final, decklist required and no proxies. With at least 150 players, 75 advance to Day 2, otherwise 50.
- **Sunday 31**: side event for players who do not qualify for Day 2.

Prices, tickets and hotels have not been announced yet: we will update this page when they are on the official website. Contact: verein@vtes.de.
