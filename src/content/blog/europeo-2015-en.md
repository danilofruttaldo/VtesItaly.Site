The 2015 edition of the _Vampire: The Eternal Struggle_ **European Championship** was played in **Warsaw** (Poland) from 28 to 30 August 2015. European Champion: **Marcin W.** (Poland).

The final table also had the Italian players **Danilo T.** and **Giulio D.**

## The final table

| Place    | Player        | Country | Deck                           |
| -------- | ------------- | ------- | ------------------------------ |
| Champion | Marcin W.     | Poland  | Goratrix Auspex/Animalism wall |
| Finalist | Pierre T.     | France  | Euro Brujah                    |
| Finalist | Marius I.     | France  | Toreador AAA                   |
| Finalist | **Danilo T.** | Italy   | Mistress Fanchon/Luna vote     |
| Finalist | **Giulio D.** | Italy   | Brujah Obfuscate vote          |
