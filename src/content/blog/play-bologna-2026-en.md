On **May 22, 23 and 24, 2026** the Italian community of _Vampire: The Eternal Struggle_ was at **Play – Festival del Gioco** at **BolognaFiere** with **open demo tables** for anyone who wants to step into the longest-running political card game in the hobby.

Three days, one invitation: **come and try VTES**. We were in **Hall 18**, in the **Vampire: The Masquerade area** (booth **J11 - Camarilla Italia** / **J13 - Area Vampiri**). Whether you had never seen the game or had only heard about it, you found a table ready, the decks already set up, and players from the Italian community to walk you through a 30–45 minute taster round. No registration, no deck to bring: you just sat down.

The booth was staffed in shifts by **players from different cities**: a chance to meet who plays near you and find out where the game runs in your area.

## Where to find us: Hall 18

**VTES** is the meaner cousin of **Vampire: The Masquerade**: same factions, same World of Darkness, same political intrigue, but on the card table, with five vampires bleeding, voting, blocking and fighting each other. If you spot the VTM community booths, you're already there.

> [!NOTE]
> **Play** is Italy's largest festival dedicated to board, role-playing and card games: three days at BolognaFiere with over 200 exhibitors, 3,000 free-play tables and 700 events. On Saturday the 23rd the fair stays open until 23:00. For tickets, map and full programme visit [play-festival.it](https://www.play-festival.it/).

See you at the table.
