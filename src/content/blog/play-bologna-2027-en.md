On **April 23, 24 and 25, 2027** the Italian community of _Vampire: The Eternal Struggle_ is back at **Play – Festival del Gioco** at **BolognaFiere** with **open demo tables** for anyone who wants to step into the longest-running political card game in the hobby.

Three days, one invitation: **come and try VTES**. Whether you've never seen the game or you've only heard about it, you'll find a table ready, the decks already set up, and players from the Italian community to walk you through a 30–45 minute taster round. No registration, no deck to bring: just sit down.

The booth will be staffed in shifts by **players from different cities**: a chance to meet who plays near you and find out where the game runs in your area.

**VTES** is the meaner cousin of **Vampire: The Masquerade**: same factions, same World of Darkness, same political intrigue, but on the card table, with five vampires bleeding, voting, blocking and fighting each other.

> [!NOTE]
> The fair is open on all three days from 9:00 to 19:00. The hall and booth for the 2027 edition have not been announced yet: we'll update this page as soon as Play publishes the map and programme. For tickets and news visit [play-festival.it](https://www.play-festival.it/).

See you at the table.
