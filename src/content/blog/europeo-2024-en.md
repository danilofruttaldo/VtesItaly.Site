The 2024 edition of the _Vampire: The Eternal Struggle_ **European Championship** was played in **Warsaw** (Poland) from 27 to 29 September 2024: the Last Chance Qualifier on Friday (**153 players**), Day 1 on Saturday (**168**), Day 2 on Sunday (**75**).
