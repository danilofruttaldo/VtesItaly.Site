**Super Sloth**: _They came for the Sloth. Now they're here for…_ The side event of the **6th stage of the [Italian Tour 2026](/en/tour/)** (Sloth), on Sunday, **November 15, 2026** at **Casa dei Giochi** in Milan, the day after the stage.

Easy to reach by **Metro 1 (Red Line)**, stop _Villa San Giovanni_ (direction Sesto San Giovanni).

## Lunch and breaks

Inside the venue there is a **bar** for food and drinks.

Lunch is planned **between the first and second round**, roughly from **12:00/12:30** to **13:30/13:45**. A **fixed-price menu** (first course + main + side) is available at a nearby pizzeria/trattoria for about **€15**, booking on the morning of the tournament.
